| Week | Channel | Content | Goal |

| Week -4 | Blog | Problem-awareness article | SEO, establish authority |
| Week -4 | LinkedIn | Teaser post about problem space | Awareness |
| Week -3 | Blog | "How we built X" engineering post | Developer community interest |
| Week -3 | Email | Waitlist announcement | Build pre-launch list |

| Week -2 | Twitter/X | Daily countdown thread | Buzz |
| Week -1 | Email | Early access invitation to waitlist | Exclusivity, conversion |
